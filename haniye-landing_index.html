About

I'm a Product Manager who thinks like a builder and supports like an engineer. My job is to take something complicated — a banking workflow, a broken funnel, a stack of vague requirements — and break it into pieces small enough to actually solve.

I've spent five years inside FinTech: automating merchant onboarding, optimizing purchase funnels, running UAT on card and wallet products, and sitting in the space between stakeholders and engineers where most real problems live. I'm comfortable in Jira writing acceptance criteria, and just as comfortable in Postman debugging why an API call is failing.

I don't fear experiments or mistakes — every failed attempt is data that moves me closer to a better answer. That mindset is the same one I bring to a backlog, a recipe, or a new hobby.
